About PMSSS

The Prime Minister's Special Scholarship Scheme was launched to provide equal opportunities to students from the Union Territories of Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh. This portal uses advanced Computer Vision and OCR technology to verify your documents automatically — eliminating paperwork and ensuring fast, transparent processing.

  • ✅ Fully digital, paperless application process
  • ✅ Automated document verification via Computer Vision / OCR
  • ✅ Real-time eligibility assessment & application tracking
  • ✅ Covers Engineering, Medical, Management & other professional courses
